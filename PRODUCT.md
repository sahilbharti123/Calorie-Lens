# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who want one private, dependable place to track everyday nutrition, hydration, health signals, weight, and strength training. They may log while eating, walking, or training, often one-handed and with limited attention. The product is especially attentive to Indian foods, mixed English/Hindi/Hinglish input, wearable health data, and users who want useful guidance without giving up their data or maintaining a subscription.

## Product Purpose

Vigorly turns quick typed or spoken descriptions into reviewable fitness records, keeps nutrition and strength training in one daily ledger, and makes the uncertainty behind every estimate visible. Success means a user can log in seconds, trust where the numbers came from, understand what to do next, and keep using the complete product offline without an account.

## Brand Personality

Focused, energetic, and evidence-led. The "Midnight Athlete" product language should feel precise and motivating without becoming clinical, judgmental, or performative. It earns confidence through transparent calculation, calm explanations, and responsive interaction rather than hype.

## Anti-references

- Calorie trackers that present guessed values as exact facts or hide calculation sources.
- AI-first products that fabricate answers when the input is ambiguous.
- Subscription-first fitness apps that withhold core tracking or require an account before the user can begin.
- Generic neon-on-black dashboards that use glow, cards, and animated metrics as decoration rather than to clarify progress.
- Overloaded all-in-one fitness products where feature breadth makes the daily logging loop feel slow.

## Design Principles

- Make the next useful action obvious within a few seconds.
- Show confidence, ranges, sources, and assumptions wherever precision is limited.
- Keep the complete daily loop fast, local-first, and interruption-safe.
- Reveal advanced training and analysis features progressively so beginners are not punished for the product's depth.
- Use motivation as feedback on real behaviour, never as guilt or fabricated certainty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA-equivalent behaviour across native and web surfaces. Preserve readable contrast, 44-point touch targets, screen-reader names and states, logical focus order, Dynamic Type resilience, non-colour status cues, and reduced-motion alternatives. Fitness, nutrition, body-weight, and adherence language should remain neutral and non-judgmental.
