# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People building sustainable nutrition, activity, and strength habits in ordinary life: beginners, experienced lifters, culturally diverse eaters, privacy-conscious users, and people who depend on iPhone, Apple Watch, VoiceOver, large text, voice input, or intermittent connectivity. Their primary job is to log accurately with minimal friction, understand progress, and continue the same workout or plan across devices.

## Product Purpose

Vigorly is a private fitness companion for meals, hydration, body-weight goals, training plans, live workouts, Apple Health, and Apple Watch. Success means the user can record or correct real-life data quickly, trust where each value came from, and see a useful plan that responds to their target weight and preferences without pretending to provide medical care.

## Brand Personality

Focused, energetic, trustworthy. The product should feel athletic and premium without becoming aggressive, clinical, childish, or ornamental. Copy is direct and calm; confidence comes from clear actions, honest estimates, and visible state.

## Anti-references

- Text-heavy screens that explain the interface before letting the user act.
- Unpolished fitness apps with hidden gestures, fixed-height clipped controls, keyboard traps, or ambiguous sync.
- Decorative dashboard mockups whose charts, cards, and glows do not improve a real task.
- Competitor dark patterns: surprise paywalls, unclear subscriptions, data loss, unreliable Watch sync, or inaccessible logging controls.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next useful action and current state before explanation.
2. Make every logged value correctable, reversible, and attributable.
3. Treat phone, Watch, Health, voice, and cloud as one coherent product with honest sync state.
4. Personalize recommendations from explicit user goals and constraints; never hide assumptions.
5. Prefer familiar controls, strong hierarchy, and visual progress over dense prose or decorative novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and meaningful VoiceOver labels throughout. Support Dynamic Type without overlapping, clipping, or hiding required actions; preserve scroll access when content grows. Respect reduced motion, do not encode state by color alone, keep touch targets at least 44 points, and provide keyboard alternatives whenever voice, Health, or Watch features are unavailable.
